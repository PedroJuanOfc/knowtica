## About

Knowtica is an AI-driven news aggregation platform focused on technology and artificial intelligence.

The idea came from a simple problem: staying up to date with tech news takes time, and balancing work, study, and daily life doesn't always leave enough room for that.

To solve this, Knowtica uses AI agents to monitor trusted technology news sources throughout the day. The system collects articles, evaluates their relevance using a scoring process, and identifies topics that are being discussed across multiple sources.

When a topic reaches a minimum relevance score, the platform generates different versions of the content:

- Short version – key information and highlights.
- Medium version – a balanced summary with additional context.
- Long version – a more complete article with deeper explanations.

All generated content includes references and links to the original sources.
